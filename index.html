TunerDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy covers TunerDeck TV, TunerDeck Mobile, TunerDeck for HDHomeRun, and TunerDeck HDHomeRun Mobile, developed by Ozzie Feliciano. Contact: ozziefel@felpfe.com.

Your devices and servers

TunerDeck connects to a DVR server or HDHomeRun device you select. It stores connection preferences, favorites, recent channels, reminders and playback state in app storage. DVR features can send searches, playback progress, favorites and recording commands to your selected server. A random client identifier, device name/model/platform, app version and connection requests help the server identify clients. Your server or service provider can also receive your IP address and retain activity according to its configuration or policy.

Streaming and metadata services

When you enable a free streaming service, request metadata, play media or open a title in another app, the relevant provider and its delivery partners receive the requests needed to provide that function. These may include your IP address, random device/client identifiers, search terms and selected content. Services may include Pluto TV, Tubi, Plex, XUMO Play, TMDB and other providers configured on your server. Availability and provider data practices are governed by their own policies. Streams may contain advertising. TunerDeck does not operate these providers.

Microphone and installed apps

Voice search is optional and uses Android's speech-recognition service. That service may process microphone audio remotely under its own privacy policy; recognized text is used for your search. TunerDeck does not maintain an audio recording archive. You can deny microphone permission and use text search. Supported Discovery features check available streaming apps to offer relevant launch destinations.

Reminders and permissions

Notifications and alarm permissions support reminders you create. Network and discovery permissions locate your selected devices. You can revoke permissions in Android Settings, disable optional sources, or remove reminders in the app. Some functions will then be unavailable.

Retention and deletion

App preferences and reminders remain until you change them, clear app data or uninstall; Android backup settings may affect restoration. Clearing app data does not delete recordings, server logs, provider accounts or information already held by your DVR or another service. Manage those through the respective server or provider. If you contact support, your message and contact details are used to respond and retained as needed to resolve the request. Contact the email above for assistance or deletion requests relating to information held by the developer.

Security

Public services commonly use HTTPS, but local tuners and user-selected DVR endpoints can use unencrypted HTTP. Use a trusted network and a secure remote-access arrangement. No transmission or storage system is guaranteed secure. TunerDeck has no built-in developer analytics or crash-reporting service; third-party servers and providers can maintain their own logs.

Children and changes

The app is a general TV client and can display content supplied by your sources. Parents should manage access to devices, channels and services. TunerDeck does not knowingly solicit children's personal information. Contact support if you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer. We may update this policy as the apps change; the latest published version will show its update date.
